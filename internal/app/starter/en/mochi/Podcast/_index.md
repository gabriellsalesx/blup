%%
This folder is the blog's Podcast page, turned on in Blup Studio (in Sections). It gathers the posts' narrations as episodes, newest first, with a player that moves on to the next one by itself, and publishes a feed for Spotify, Apple Podcasts and other apps (its address shows on the Studio's Podcast screen).

The episodes are the posts with a narration: make the narration on each post, in the properties panel. Each episode's cover is the post's cover. The podcast's details (author, contact e-mail, category, explicit content and the podcast's cover) are this note's properties, and the Studio's Podcast screen edits all of them.

The text above this comment shows under the page's title, as in the other folders. The Description property is something else: it is for Google, sharing the link and the feed (empty, the text above counts).
%%
