Every site published by Blup can be **installed as an app**, on phones and computers, and it **opens offline**. There's nothing to turn on.

## Install

- **Android and computers (Chrome, Edge):** an **Install app** button shows in the site's footer, and the browser also offers the install icon in the address bar.
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** tap **Share** and then **Add to Home Screen**.

The app opens full screen, with the site's icon and name.

## The icon

Blup makes the icons for each device from the site's **favicon**: Android, iPhone, Android's round cropped icon and the `favicon.ico`. To change it, change the favicon on the Studio's **Site** screen (see [Set up the site](Set%20up%20the%20site.md)). A square image, at least 512 pixels or in SVG, looks best.

## Offline

On the first visit, the browser keeps all the pages, styles and fonts. After that, the site opens even without internet.

- **Photos** are kept as the person views them, so they don't all download at once.
- **Comments and reactions** need internet; without it, the rest of the page works as usual.
- On every publish, the browser downloads the new version by itself.

The preview (the Studio's or `blup preview`) doesn't become an app, since it changes all the time; only the published site does.
