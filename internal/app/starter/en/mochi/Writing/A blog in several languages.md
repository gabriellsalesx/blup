The blog can speak Portuguese, English and Spanish. You write in the site's main language, and the Studio translates each post into the others. The site gets a **language picker** at the top, and Google understands which page is in which language.

## Translate a post

1. Open the post in the Studio.
2. In the **Properties** panel, go to **Languages**. The original and each language are there.
3. Click **Translate** on the language you want.

The AI translates the title, the description and the text. Code, links, images and the callout types don't change. The translation goes into a `pt` or `es` (or `en`) folder next to the post and becomes the page for that language on the site. Click **Open** to see and edit the translation like any other note.

## Translate the whole site

The **Languages** screen, in the side menu, shows how many notes are translated, out of date and not translated in each language. **Translate what is missing** translates everything at once.

Translation also takes care of what shows up outside the posts: the site's description, the button text, the names in the menu and the sections, the tags and the authors' role and bio.

## When the original changes

Changed the post after translating it? The translation shows as **Out of date**. Click **Translate again**.

## The "Translated with AI" badge

A translation made by the AI shows, next to the title, the **Translated with AI** badge, with a link to the original. Read it and liked it? In the Languages panel, click **Mark as reviewed**: the badge goes away.

## Who translates

In the Studio's **Settings**, under **AI and voice**, choose who translates:

- **On the computer**: free and offline, with the same translator Firefox uses. It downloads once, and the notes never leave the computer.
- **AI in the cloud**: through your Blup Cloud plan (from Pro up) or your own OpenRouter key. The translation reads a bit more naturally.

## Comments and narration

Comments and reactions are the same in every language: people reading in English and in Portuguese talk on the same page, and each comment shows the language it was written in. The narration of a translated post comes out in the translation's language. See [Narrate a post as audio](Narrate%20a%20post%20as%20audio.md).

> [!info] Translations are notes
> Translation and the language picker are set up in the Studio. Translations are ordinary notes, so you can review and edit them afterwards.
