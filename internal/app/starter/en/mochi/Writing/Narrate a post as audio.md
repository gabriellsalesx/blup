With narration, people visiting the blog can **listen** to the post instead of reading it: while driving, cooking or with tired eyes. The site shows a player at the top of the post.

## Narrate

1. Open the post in the Studio.
2. In the **Properties** panel, go to **Narration** and click **Make narration**.
3. Watch the parts being made. When it finishes, the Studio shows the length and size of the audio.

Listen in the preview's player, next to it. On the next publish, the audio goes to the site.

The audio is an MP3 file, kept in the **Attachments** folder. The voice reads the title, the description and the text; code blocks, formulas, images and tables are left out.

## Changed the text?

The Studio notices when the text changed after the narration and lets you know. Click **Make it again** so the audio keeps up.

## Pick the voice

In the Studio's **Settings**, under **Narration**, pick a voice for each language: Portuguese, English and Spanish. It works for all your sites. You can listen to a sample before choosing.

The voices come from three places:

- **On the computer, free**: the voice runs on your computer, offline. It downloads once.
- **Blup Cloud, included in the plan**: from Pro up. Before narrating, the Studio shows how much of your monthly AI use the post will take.
- **OpenRouter, paid**: with your own key, spending credits from your account.

A translated post is narrated in the translation's language, with that language's voice. See [A blog in several languages](A%20blog%20in%20several%20languages.md).

> [!warning] Password protected posts
> Password protected posts can't be narrated: the audio is public and would give the text away.

## It becomes a podcast

Narrated posts can become episodes of a podcast for the blog, with a feed for Spotify and Apple Podcasts. See [Your blog becomes a podcast](../Customizing/Your%20blog%20becomes%20a%20podcast.md).

> [!info] And the command line?
> Narration is a Studio feature. Once made, it stays in the note's properties and remains on the site even when you publish from the command line.
