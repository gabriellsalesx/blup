This block is a comment (the text between the two pairs of percent signs): you only see it while editing, in the Studio or in Obsidian, and it never goes to the site.

The text above is the introduction of the home page. The properties of this note (_index) hold the settings of the whole site. The Site screen, in the Studio's side menu, edits the same ones:

- site_name and site_description: the name (browser tab, footer and Google) and one sentence about the site.
- logo and favicon: images from the Attachments folder, like [[logo.png]].
- menu: one item per line. "Writing" uses that folder; "Shop | https://example.com" becomes a link. Empty, the menu is automatic.
- social: addresses of your profiles; the icon comes from the address.
- button_text and button_link: a button at the top, like "Subscribe".

Everything is explained in the post "Set up the site".
