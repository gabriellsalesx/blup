Not every note needs to become a website. For everyday notes, Blup Studio has **note vaults**: notes just for you, that are never published.

## Create a vault

On the Studio's home screen, click **Create a note vault**, give it a name and that's it: just start writing. Sites and vaults sit side by side on the home screen, and the picker at the bottom of the side menu switches from one to the other.

## What a vault has

- **Notes** with the same editor as sites, with creation date, update date and tags.
- **Mentioned in**: under each note, the other notes that point to it.
- **Map of notes**: each note is a dot and each link a line. Good for seeing how ideas connect.
- **Canvas**: a free board with text cards, notes, links and arrows.
- **Drawings**: shapes, arrows, text and freehand drawing.
- **Databases**: lists of clients, tasks or reading, as a table or a board. Each item is a note.
- **Note of the day**: one click opens today's note, with the time, ready to write.

Type `@` to mention another note or a date, and `/` to insert a heading, a list, a table, a drawing or a canvas.

## Keep them and take them to another computer

The vault's notes are plain files in a folder on your computer. To have the same notes on another computer, use the **Sync** button: through Blup Cloud, with end-to-end encryption, or in a private GitHub repository. See [Sync the notes](../Customizing/Sync%20the%20notes.md).

> [!info] Plain files
> A vault's notes are plain Markdown files. The canvas uses the open JSON Canvas format. Drawings, databases, the map of notes and the note of the day are the Studio's own features.
