Blup Studio is a desktop app for Linux, macOS and Windows. You write, see and publish the site in the same window, and there is nothing to memorize: the Studio asks one thing at a time.

## A new site

On the home screen, click **Create a new site**. Every step comes with a suggested answer, and you can change everything later.

1. **The kind of site**: Personal blog or Product site.
2. **The theme**: for a blog, Nori (this one, a single column) or Mochi.
3. **The name**: site name, one sentence about it (optional), your name (optional) and the site language.
4. **Where the notes live**: in a new folder (the simplest way) or in a folder you choose.
5. **Review** and click **Create the site**.

If the folder is empty, Blup adds the theme's example site: this tutorial. Then click **Open the site**.

Already made a site before? Click **I already have a Blup site**. The sites you opened lately show up there too.

> [!info] Where things live
> Your notes are ordinary text files (Markdown) in a folder on your computer. Each note becomes a page. In a separate folder, Blup keeps the site settings and the files it uses to build the pages. You don't need to open that folder.

## The window

- **Files**, on the left: your notes and folders. Use **New note** and **New folder**, or search the notes by name. Right-click a note to rename, move, copy or delete it.
- **The editor**, in the middle: where you write. Saving is automatic.
- **Preview**, on the right: the site, live. It updates every time the note is saved.

The side menu has the rest: **Write**, **Look**, **Comments**, **Cookies and statistics**, **Authors**, **Attachments**, **Password**, **Publish** and **Trash**. Each one has a post in this blog.

> [!tip] Nothing gets lost
> Renaming a note updates the links that point to it. Deleting sends it to the **Trash**, in the side menu, where you can restore it.

## More than one site

The switcher at the top of the window changes sites. The gear opens the **Settings**: the Studio's language, light or dark look, the editor's text size and spell check, your accounts and the shortcuts.
