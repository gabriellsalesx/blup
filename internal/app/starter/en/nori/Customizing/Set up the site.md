Open **Look** in the side menu. Everything about how the site presents itself is there, and the preview shows each change.

## Name and description

In **Name and description**, write the site name and one sentence about it.

The name shows up in the browser tab, in the footer (© year and name) and on Google. The description shows up on Google and when someone shares the site.

The **Author** field sets who signs the posts that don't name an author.

## Logo and favicon

- **Logo**: shows at the top of the site. Without one, the name shows.
- **Favicon**: the icon of the browser tab and of the app. Square, ideally 512 pixels, or an SVG, which stays sharp at any size.

Click **Choose image** to pick each one. Blup creates the sizes browsers and phones ask for.

## Top menu

The menu's pages, in order:

- **Add page** picks a folder or note of the site. "Writing", for example, leads to the Writing section.
- **Add link** adds a link to another site, like a shop.
- The names are up to you: a menu item can say "Reading" and lead to the Books folder.
- **Move up** and **Move down** change the order.

Without any item, the menu is automatic: the folders at the root of the site.

## Top button

A highlighted button to the right of the menu, like "Subscribe". Write the **Button text** and **Where it leads**. Leave the text empty for no button.

## Social links

Click **Add network** and paste the address of each profile, or an e-mail. The icons show up in the footer. Blup recognizes the network from the address: Instagram, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, GitHub, Facebook, WhatsApp and e-mail.

> [!tip] Where it is saved
> These settings are properties of the **_index** note, at the root of the site folder. Open it in the Studio: this blog's menu is there too.
