This folder is the blog's Podcast page, turned on in Blup Studio (in Sections). It gathers the posts' narrations as episodes, newest first, with a player that moves on to the next one by itself, and publishes a feed for Spotify, Apple Podcasts and other apps (the address shows on the Studio's Podcast screen).

The episodes are the posts with a narration: make the narration on each post, in the Properties panel. Each episode's cover is the post's cover. Hidden posts, drafts and password protected posts are left out.

The podcast's details are this note's properties, and the Studio's Podcast screen edits all of them:

- podcast_title: the show's name. Empty, the site's name is used.
- description: the description for Google, link sharing and the feed. Empty, the text above is used.
- podcast_author: the author. Empty, the site's author is used.
- podcast_email: the contact e-mail. It is public in the feed: that is how Spotify and Apple confirm the podcast is yours.
- podcast_category: the Apple Podcasts category, with its English name (Technology, Arts, Education...).
- podcast_explicit: true when there is adult language or themes.
- image: the podcast's cover, a square from 1400 to 3000 px. Empty, the newest episode's cover is used.

The text above this comment shows under the page's title, as in the other folders.
%%
