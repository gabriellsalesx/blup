title: Writers
blup: authors
---
One note per author, named after the person. In the Studio, the **Authors** screen, in the side menu, takes care of them: photo, role, social networks and bio. In Obsidian, the properties are **photo** (image), **role** (job title) and **social** (profile links), and the note's text is the bio. In posts, pick the author in the **Authors** field of the Properties, or write `authors: [Name]`.

This folder doesn't become a page on the site.
