Blup Studio uses AI where it saves you work, and you choose where it comes from.

## Post narration

One click on **Make narration**, in the post's properties, and a voice reads the text. The audio goes on the page with a player, so visitors can listen instead of reading. On blogs, the narrations also become podcast episodes.

## Note translation

In each note's **Languages** panel, click **Translate**. Blup translates the text and leaves code, links and images untouched. If the original changes, it tells you the translation fell behind.

## Agents in the terminal

The Studio has a terminal open in your notes folder. From there you call the AI agent you already use, like Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Gemini CLI or Antigravity. Before the agent starts, the Studio keeps a copy of your notes, so you can undo what it changed.

## Where the AI comes from

| Option | Who it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Blup Cloud AI | People on a paid plan, no key to set up |
| OpenRouter key | People who already have an account there |
| On your computer | People who want it free: the voice and the translation run on your machine |
