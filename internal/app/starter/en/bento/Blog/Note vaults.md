Not every note needs to become a page. For everyday notes, Blup Studio has **note vaults**: nothing in them is published.

## What a vault has

- **Note of the day**: one click opens today's note, ready to write.
- **Canvas**: spread notes and cards on a board and connect them with arrows.
- **Drawings**: shapes, arrows, text and free drawing, with PNG export.
- **Databases**: clients, tasks or reading, as a table or a board. Each item is a note.
- **Map of notes**: see how your notes connect. And the **Mentioned in** panel shows which notes mention each one.

## Side by side with your sites

Sites and vaults sit side by side on the Studio's start screen. Vaults also sync through Blup Cloud, with the same end-to-end encryption.

To make yours, see [What is a note vault?](../Help/Note%20vaults/What%20is%20a%20note%20vault.md)
