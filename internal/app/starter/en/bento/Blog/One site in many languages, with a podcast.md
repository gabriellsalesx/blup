## Many languages

A Blup site is written in one language and can also be published in the others: Portuguese, English and Spanish.

1. In the note, open the **Languages** panel and translate.
2. For the whole site, the **Languages** screen shows what is missing and translates everything at once.

Visitors get a language switcher on every translated page. Google understands that the versions are the same page. And comments, votes and reactions add up readers from every language.

Pages translated by AI show a small badge. After you review one, mark the translation as reviewed, and the badge goes away.

## Podcast

On blogs (the Mochi and Nori themes), the **Podcast** section gathers the narrated posts as episodes:

- a page with the list of episodes and a player that moves on to the next one by itself;
- a feed to submit to Spotify, Apple Podcasts and other apps;
- one feed per language, if the blog is published in more than one.

To get started, see [How to create a podcast?](../Help/Blup%20Studio/How%20to%20create%20a%20podcast.md)
