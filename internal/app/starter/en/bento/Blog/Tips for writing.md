## A one-sentence description

The description shows up in lists, on Google and when someone shares the page. One clear sentence beats a cut-off first paragraph.

## Headings with ##

Use `##` and `###` to split the text. They become the **On this page** table of contents and get their own link, handy for sending someone the exact part.

## Keep the preview open

In the Studio, leave the **Preview** open: every change shows up right away. In the command line, run `blup preview`.

## Callouts

Callouts draw attention to what matters. In the Studio, type `/` and choose **Callout**.

> [!tip] Like this
> A callout with a tip, a warning or some information.

## Reminders for yourself

Text between `%%` and `%%` stays in the note and doesn't go to the site.

## Publish often

Blup only sends what changed. Publishing a little and often is calmer than saving everything for one big update.
