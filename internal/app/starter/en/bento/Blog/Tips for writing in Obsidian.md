## A one-sentence description

The **description** property shows up in lists, on Google and when someone shares the page. One clear sentence beats a cut-off first paragraph.

## Headings with ##

Use `##` and `###` to split the text. They become the **On this page** table of contents and get their own link, handy for sending someone the exact part.

## Preview open on the side

With `blup preview` open, every note you save shows up instantly. You write and you see.

## Templates

The **Templates** folder has templates with the right properties for each section. Use **Templates: Insert template**.

## Comments for yourself

Reminders between `%%` and `%%` stay in the note and don't go to the site.

## Publish often

`blup publish` sends only what changed. Publishing a little and often is calmer than saving it all up.
