---
title: How do I publish the site from Blup Studio?
description: The Publish screen does it all, from picking the place to the live address.
weight: 2
---
1. Click **Publish**, at the top of the Studio.
2. Choose where to publish: **Blup Cloud**, **Cloudflare Workers** or **GitHub Pages**.
3. For Blup Cloud, sign in to your account and pick the address. For the others, connect your account by following the steps on the screen.
4. Click **Publish** and watch the progress.

At the end, the Studio shows the site's address.

## On the command line

Run `blup publish`. The first time, it asks where to publish and asks for the access key. After that, it remembers your choice.
