---
title: What goes on the site and what stays just with me?
description: Only what you published goes. Everything else stays on your computer.
weight: 5
---
A site is public by nature: anyone with the address can open it. That's why Blup only sends what was made to be published.

- **Goes on the site**: the notes in the site folder and the attachments they use.
- **Stays out**: drafts, reminders between `%%` and notes outside the site folder.
- **Password-protected posts**: leave your computer encrypted. Without the password, nobody can read them.

Note vaults never become a site.
