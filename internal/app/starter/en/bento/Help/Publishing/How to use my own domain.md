---
title: How do I use my own domain?
description: An address like mysite.com instead of the service's address. With Blup Cloud, on the Max and Ultra plans.
weight: 4
---
A custom domain is linked only through **Blup Cloud**, on the **Max** and **Ultra** plans.

1. Publish the site with Blup Cloud once.
2. On the **Publish** screen, in the **Own domain** block, enter the domain and click **Link**.
3. The Studio shows the records you need to create where you bought the domain.
4. Click **Check** and wait for **Active**. The Studio publishes again on its own, so the links use the new domain.

The full steps are in [Custom domain](../../Docs/Publishing/Custom%20domain.md).

## On Cloudflare Workers and GitHub Pages

Sites published with those services stay at their free address, like `mysite.youraccount.workers.dev` or `yourname.github.io/mysite`. Blup doesn't link a custom domain there.
