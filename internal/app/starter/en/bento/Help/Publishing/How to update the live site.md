---
title: How do I update a site that is already live?
description: Write, check, publish.
weight: 3
---
Click **Publish** again (or run `blup publish`). Blup builds the site and sends only the files that changed, so updates are quick.

If nothing changed since last time, it tells you and sends nothing.

> [!tip] Publish little and often
> Publishing after each change is easier than saving everything up for one day.
