---
title: Where to publish?
description: Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages, both free.
weight: 1
---
| | Cloudflare Pages | GitHub Pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | Free |
| Address | `mysite.pages.dev` | `yourname.github.io/mysite` |
| Account | dash.cloudflare.com | github.com |

Choose one the first time you run `blup publish`. Blup remembers your choice.
