---
title: How do I use the note of the day?
description: One click opens today's note, ready to write.
weight: 2
---
1. In a vault, click **Note of the day** in the left bar (or press `Ctrl+Alt+D`).
2. The Studio opens today's note, in the **Daily** folder, and puts the cursor at the end.
3. Each time you open it, it adds a heading with the current time.

This way your day is recorded in blocks, one below the other, without creating anything by hand.
