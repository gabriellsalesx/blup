---
title: What is a note vault?
description: A place for your own notes, which never becomes a site.
weight: 1
---
A note vault keeps notes just for you: ideas, meetings, study notes, lists. It has no theme and no publishing, so nothing in it goes on the internet.

Besides notes, a vault has:

- **Note of the day**, to write a journal or today's tasks;
- **Canvas**, to spread notes and cards on a board and connect them;
- **Drawings**, with shapes, arrows, text and free drawing;
- **Databases**, like a list of clients or tasks;
- **Map of notes**, which shows how your notes link to each other.

Vaults are a Blup Studio feature. The notes are still Markdown files, so any text editor opens them too.

## How to create one

On the Studio's home screen, under **Note vaults**, click **Create a note vault**, give it a name and confirm.
