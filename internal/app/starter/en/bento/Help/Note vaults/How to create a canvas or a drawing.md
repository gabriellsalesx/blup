---
title: How do I create a canvas or a drawing?
description: Free boards to organize ideas and sketch.
weight: 3
---
In a vault's left bar, click **New canvas** or **New drawing**. The file is created with the date in its name and opens right away. You can also create one from the `/` menu inside a note, which also puts a link to it in the text.

## On the canvas

- Double-click to create a text card.
- Drag notes in from the sidebar: they become cards.
- Pull the dot on the side of a card to another card to draw an arrow.

## In the drawing

Pick the tool (rectangle, diamond, ellipse, arrow, line, free drawing, text or eraser), the color and the thickness. When you are done, export it as a PNG to use in a note.

Both save on their own and have a full screen mode.
