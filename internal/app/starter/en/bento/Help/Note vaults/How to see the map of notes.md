---
title: How do I see the map of notes?
description: A picture of how your notes link to each other.
weight: 5
---
In a vault, click **Map of notes** in the left bar (or press `Ctrl+Alt+G`).

- Each note is a dot, each link is a line.
- Hover to highlight a note and its neighbors. Click to open it.
- In **Map settings**, filter by folder or tag, color groups and change the forces of the drawing.

## Mentioned in

At the bottom of each note in the vault, the **Mentioned in** panel lists the notes that link to it, with the passage of each mention.
