---
title: How do I create a database?
description: Lists of clients, tasks or reading, where each item is a note.
weight: 4
---
1. In a vault, click **New database** in the left bar.
2. Pick a template: **Clients**, **Tasks**, **Reading** or **From scratch**.
3. Give it a name and create it.

## Ways to view it

- **Table**: you edit each cell like in a spreadsheet.
- **Board**: columns by status; drag a card to another column to change it.

Each record is a note: click its name to open it and write as much as you like. The fields show in the note's properties.

## Columns

Click a column header to change its name, its kind (text, number, choice, date, phone, e-mail, link, yes/no, link to a note) and its options.
