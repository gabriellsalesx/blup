---
title: Why is the address I want not available?
description: On Blup Cloud, each getblup.site address belongs to a single site.
weight: 5
---
As you type the address on the **Publish** screen, the Studio asks Blup Cloud whether it is free. What you may see:

- **Already taken**: another site already has that name. The Studio suggests similar names that are free; tap one to use it.
- **Reserved**: Blup keeps that name, so it can't be used.
- **Not valid**: use only lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.

Take your time choosing: after the first publish, the address stays fixed.
