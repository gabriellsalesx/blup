---
title: Why did the note change while I was writing?
description: Another program or an AI agent changed the same file.
weight: 6
---
The Studio notices when a note changes outside it: in another editor, in an AI agent in the terminal or through a sync.

- If you hadn't changed anything, the note reloads on its own.
- If you were editing, the Studio tells you there was an outside change and lets you reload when you want.

This way nothing gets overwritten without you seeing it. If the change came from an AI agent, you can undo it from the terminal.
