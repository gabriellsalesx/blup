---
title: Why is there a note with "conflict" in its name?
description: The same note was edited on two computers.
weight: 4
---
While syncing, the same note had changed here and on another computer. So nothing gets lost, Blup kept both:

- the note with the normal name is **your** version;
- the note with `(conflict date time)` in its name is the **other** one.

Compare the two, bring what you want into the main note and delete the copy. On the next sync, everything matches on both computers.
