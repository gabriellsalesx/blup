---
title: Why is the access key not working?
description: An expired key or one without the right permission.
weight: 3
---
Blup checks the key before using it. If it doesn't work, Blup explains why and asks for another one.

- **GitHub**: when creating the key, under **Repository access**, choose **All repositories**.
- **Cloudflare**: the key needs the **Account › Workers Scripts › Edit** permission.
- Keys expire. Create a new one the same way and paste it when Blup asks.

If you publish with Blup Cloud, you don't need a key: just sign in to your account.
