---
title: My first site in 5 minutes
description: From the Obsidian vault to a site online.
weight: 2
---
1. Run `blup` and choose the language.
2. Choose the vault and the folder that becomes the site.
3. Choose the theme, name the site and press Enter on the suggestions.
4. Check the site in the preview.
5. Run `blup publish` and choose Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages.

Done: Blup shows your site's address.
