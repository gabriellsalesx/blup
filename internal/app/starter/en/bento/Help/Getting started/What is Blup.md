---
title: What is Blup?
description: A simple way to turn notes into sites and to keep your own notes.
weight: 1
---
Blup turns a folder of notes into a fast, good-looking site. You write in Markdown and Blup takes care of the rest: it builds the pages, optimizes the images and puts the site online.

It does two kinds of things:

- **Sites**: blogs (Mochi and Nori themes) and product sites with docs, roadmap, changelog, help center and blog (Bento theme, this one).
- **Note vaults**: notes just for you, which never become a site, with canvases, drawings, databases and a note of the day.

## Where do I write?

The recommended way is **Blup Studio**, an app for Linux, macOS and Windows with an editor, a preview of the site right beside it and screens to set everything up.

If you already use **Obsidian**, you can keep using it. The notes are plain Markdown files, and the same folder opens in both. To build and publish without the Studio, there is the `blup` command line.
