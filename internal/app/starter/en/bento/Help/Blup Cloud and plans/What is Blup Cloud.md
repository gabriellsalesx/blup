---
title: What is Blup Cloud?
description: One account to sync your notes, publish sites and use Blup's AI.
weight: 1
---
Blup Cloud is your Blup account in the cloud. It works for all your sites and vaults, on all your computers.

With it you can:

- **Sync your notes** between computers, with end-to-end encryption.
- **Publish sites** at `yoursite.getblup.site`, with nothing to set up (paid plans).
- **Use your plan's AI** to narrate posts and translate notes, without a key of your own (paid plans).
- Keep a **version history** of your notes (paid plans).

To sign in, open **Settings** in the Studio and click **Sign in to Blup Cloud**. Signing in happens in the browser.
