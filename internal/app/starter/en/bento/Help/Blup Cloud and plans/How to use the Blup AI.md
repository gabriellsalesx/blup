title: How do I use Blup's AI?
description: Narration and translation through your plan, your OpenRouter key or on your computer.

---
Blup Studio uses AI for two things: **narrating posts** as audio and **translating notes**. There are three ways to turn it on:

| Way | Cost | How to turn it on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blup Cloud AI | Included from the Pro plan up | Sign in to your Blup Cloud account |
| Your OpenRouter key | Whatever OpenRouter charges | Settings, AI tab |
| On your computer | Free | Download the voice or the translation in Settings |

In **Settings**, on the **Blup Cloud** tab, you can see how much of this month's AI you have used.

On top of that, the Studio's terminal opens AI agents you already have installed, like Claude Code or Codex. See [How do I use an AI agent in the Studio's terminal?](../Blup%20Studio/How%20to%20use%20an%20AI%20agent%20in%20the%20terminal.md)
