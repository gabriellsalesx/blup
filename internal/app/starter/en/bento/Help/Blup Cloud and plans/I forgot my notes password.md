---
title: I forgot my notes password. What now?
description: Use the recovery key Blup showed you when you created the password.
weight: 4
---
On Blup Cloud, your notes are encrypted on your computer before they are uploaded. Not even Blup can read them. That's why there are two ways to open them:

- the **notes password**, which you created;
- the **recovery key**, shown only once, when you created the password.

If you forgot the password, use the recovery key to get in and create a new password.

> [!warning] Keep your recovery key safe
> Without the password and without the key, nobody can recover the notes in the cloud, not even Blup. The notes already on your computer stay there.
