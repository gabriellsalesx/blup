---
title: How do I hide a note from the site?
description: Draft, hidden page, password-protected page or off the site for good.
weight: 4
---
In Blup Studio, open the note's **Properties** and check the option. In Obsidian, write the property.

| I want the note to... | In the Studio | In Obsidian |
| --- | --- | --- |
| stay off the site for now | Draft | `draft: true` |
| be on the site, but out of the lists and Google | Hidden | `hidden: true` |
| ask for a password | Password | `password: your-password` |
| never be published | Keep it outside the site folder | Keep it outside the site folder |

> [!info] The password really protects it
> The text of a password-protected post leaves your computer encrypted. Without the password, nobody can read it, not even by looking at the page's code.
