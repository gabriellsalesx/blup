---
title: How to hide a note from the site
description: Draft, hidden page or off the site for good.
weight: 3
---
| I want the note to... | Use |
| --- | --- |
| stay off the site for now | `draft: true` |
| be on the site, but out of lists and Google | `hidden: true` |
| ask for a password | `password: your-password` |
| never be published | Keep it outside the site folder |
