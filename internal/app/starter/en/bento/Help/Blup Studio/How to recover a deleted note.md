---
title: How do I recover a deleted note?
description: From Blup's Trash or from the daily copies.
weight: 7
---
## From the Trash

1. Open **Trash** in the left bar.
2. Find the note and click **Restore**.

It goes back to where it was. If there is already another note with the same name there, the Studio doesn't overwrite it.

## From the daily copies

In **Settings**, on the **General** tab, turn on **Daily copy of the notes**. The Studio keeps one `.zip` a day, with the last 7 copies, in a folder you choose.

## From the Blup Cloud history

On the paid plans, syncing keeps older versions of your notes: 1 month on Pro and 12 months on Max and Ultra.
