---
title: How do I translate the site into another language?
description: Portuguese, English and Spanish, note by note or all at once.
weight: 3
---
## One note

Open the note and, in the **Languages** panel, click **Translate** for the language you want. The translation becomes a new note, in a folder named with the language code. If the original changes, the panel tells you and offers **Translate again**.

## The whole site

Open the **Languages** screen. It shows how many notes are translated, out of date or missing in each language, and translates what is missing in one go.

## Who translates

- **On the computer**: free, after you download the local translation in the settings.
- **Blup Cloud AI**: included in the paid plans.
- **Your OpenRouter key**: connect it in the AI settings.

The site shows a "Translated with AI" badge on translated pages. Once you have reviewed a page, use **Mark as reviewed** in the Languages panel, and the badge goes away.

## What visitors see

Each translated page gets a language switcher, and Google understands the versions are the same page. A link to a note that has no translation yet leads to the original. Comments, votes and reactions add up readers from every language.
