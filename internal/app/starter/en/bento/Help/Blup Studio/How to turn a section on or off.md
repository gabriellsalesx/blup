---
title: How do I turn a section on or off?
description: The Sections screen adds and removes sections of a Bento site.
weight: 2
---
1. Open **Sections** in the left bar.
2. To add one, pick the section: docs, roadmap, changelog, help center, blog or **Feedback**. It comes with example notes.
3. To take one out, remove the section. The folder goes to Blup's Trash, and you can restore it later.

The **Feedback** section (a board of requests with votes) can only be added here.

> [!tip] In Obsidian
> Each section is a folder with an **_index** note and the `blup` property. Deleting the folder takes the section off the site.
