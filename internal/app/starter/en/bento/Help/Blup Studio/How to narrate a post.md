---
title: How do I narrate a post as audio?
description: "A voice reads the post and the site gets a player. On blogs, the audio can also become a podcast."
weight: 4
---
1. Open the post and go to **Properties**.
2. Click **Make narration**.
3. Wait for it to finish. The audio goes into the attachments, and the post's page gets a player.

If you change the text later, the Studio tells you the narration is out of date. Make it again so the audio matches.

## Which voice is used

- **Voice on the computer**: free, after you download it in the settings.
- **Blup Cloud AI**: included in the paid plans.
- **Your OpenRouter key**: connect it in the AI settings.

In **Settings**, on the **Narration** tab, you choose the voice for each language.

> [!info] Password-protected posts are not narrated
> The audio would be public and would give away what the password protects.

> [!tip] Narrations become a podcast
> On Mochi and Nori blogs, narrated posts can become episodes of a podcast. See [How do I create a podcast?](How%20to%20create%20a%20podcast.md)
