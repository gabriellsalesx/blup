---
title: Writers
blup: authors
---
One note per author, named after the person. In Blup Studio, the **Authors** screen creates and edits the profiles: photo, role, social links and bio. In Obsidian, use the properties **photo** (image), **role** and **social** (profile links); the note's text is the bio. In posts, write `authors: [Name]` or pick the author in Properties.

This folder does not become a page of the site.
