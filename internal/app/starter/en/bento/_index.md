This block is an Obsidian comment (the text between the two pairs of percent signs): you only see it while editing, never on the site.

The text above is the introduction of the home page. The properties of this note (_index) set up the whole site, and Blup ("Customize the site") edits the same ones:

- site_name and site_description: the name (browser tab, footer and Google) and one sentence about the site.
- logo and favicon: images from the vault, like [[logo.png]].
- menu: one item per line. "Docs" uses that folder; "Shop | https://example.com" becomes a link. Empty, the menu shows the sections.
- social: addresses of your profiles; the icon comes from the address.
- button_text and button_link: the button at the top right.

Everything is explained in Docs > Configuration.
