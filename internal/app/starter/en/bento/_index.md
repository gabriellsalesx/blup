This example site comes with a complete Blup tutorial. Each section below is a folder of your notes: open the notes in **Blup Studio** (the recommended way) to see how each page was made, with the site preview beside them. The notes are plain Markdown files, so any text editor opens them too. Then just edit them and replace them with your product's content.

The text above is the introduction of the home page. The properties of this note (_index) set up the whole site. In Blup Studio, you edit all of them on the Site screen, without touching this note. You can also edit them by hand in the note's properties:

- site_name and site_description: the name (browser tab, footer and Google) and one sentence about the site.
- logo and favicon: images from your notes, like [[logo.png]].
- menu: one item per line. "Docs" uses that folder; "Shop | https://example.com" becomes a link. Empty, the menu shows the sections. In the Studio, you can make submenus by dragging one item onto another.
- social: addresses of your profiles; the icon comes from the address. In the Studio, there is a button for each network.
- button_text and button_link: the highlighted button at the top right.

Everything is explained in the Docs.
