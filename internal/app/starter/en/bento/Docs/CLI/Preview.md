---
title: Preview
description: See the site in the browser while you write.
weight: 2
---
Run `blup preview`. Blup builds the site, opens the browser and watches the folder: **every note you save in Obsidian updates the page by itself**, right on the page you are editing.

- The preview also shows drafts (`draft: true`).
- Password protected posts show up locked, like on the real site.
- Search works in the preview.
- To stop, press **Ctrl+C** in the terminal.

> [!tip] Keep it open
> With Obsidian on one side and the preview on the other, you see every change instantly.
