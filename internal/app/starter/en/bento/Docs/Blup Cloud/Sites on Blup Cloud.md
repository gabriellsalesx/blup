On the paid plans, Blup Cloud also hosts your sites. The address is `name.getblup.site`, and there's no account from another service to connect.

| Plan | Sites online |
| --- | --- |
| Pro | 1 |
| Max | 5, with a custom domain |
| Ultra | 10, with a custom domain |

## Publish

1. In the Studio, click **Publish** and pick **Blup Cloud**.
2. In **Site address**, type the name that goes before `.getblup.site`: 3 to 40 lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. Accents are removed for you.
3. As you type, the Studio checks the name:
   - **Available**: you can use it.
   - **Already this site's address**: all good.
   - **Already taken**: the Studio suggests similar names that are free; tap one.
   - **Blup keeps that name**: pick another one.
4. The screen shows how many of your plan's sites are in use.
5. Click **Publish now**.

> [!warning] The address is fixed
> After the first publish, the address doesn't change. Take your time choosing.

## Fast and light

- The site is built on your computer, with its files minified and compressed.
- Only what changed is sent, so an update takes seconds.

## Privacy of what goes online

A site is public by nature: anyone with the address can read it. That's why Blup sends only what you published:

- Drafts and comments between `%%` stay on your computer.
- Of the attachments, only the ones some note uses go up.
- Posts with a password leave **your computer encrypted**: not even the host can read them without the password.
- Synced notes (the encrypted copy from [Sync your notes](Sync%20your%20notes.md)) are a different thing and never become a site.

## Custom domain

On the Max and Ultra plans, Blup Cloud sites can have a custom domain. Meanwhile, it already works with Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages. See [Custom domain](../Publishing/Custom%20domain.md).
