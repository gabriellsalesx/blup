With your notes synced through Blup Cloud, the paid plans keep the old versions of each note:

| Plan | For how long |
| --- | --- |
| Free | No history |
| Pro | 1 month |
| Max and Ultra | 12 months |

Old versions count toward your plan's storage.

## See the versions of a note

1. In the tree, right-click the note.
2. Pick **Earlier versions**.
3. Click a version to see how it was.
4. Want it back? **Bring this version back**.

Bringing a version back creates a new version: the one that was there before stays in the history. Nothing is lost.

Files that aren't text (an image, for example) can't be read there, but they can be brought back too.

## Deleted notes

Deleted a note and already emptied the Trash? Open the **Trash**: the **Deleted, kept on Blup Cloud** block lists the notes your plan's history still keeps. Click **Bring back**.

> [!tip] Other safety nets
> Even without Blup Cloud, the Studio has the Trash and a daily copy of your notes. See [Trash and version history](../Blup%20Studio/Trash%20and%20history.md).
