**Blup Cloud** is your Blup account in the cloud. With it, you keep your notes encrypted to use on other computers, publish sites at a `getblup.site` address and use AI without a key of your own. It is **optional**: everything works on this computer without it.

## Sign in

1. In the Studio, open **Settings** and click the card at the top, **Sign in to Blup Cloud**.
2. Click **Sign in with Blup**. The Studio opens Blup Cloud in the browser and shows a code.
3. In the browser, check that the code is the same and approve.
4. Done: the card shows your e-mail and your plan.

The account works for all your vaults and sites, on this computer and on the others.

## The plans

| | Free | Pro | Max | Ultra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price per month | R$ 0 | R$ 14.90 | R$ 29.90 | R$ 59.90 |
| Price per year | R$ 0 | R$ 149 | R$ 299 | R$ 599 |
| Notes in the cloud | 50 MB | 1 GB | 5 GB | 10 GB |
| When it syncs | With the button | In real time | In real time | In real time |
| Computers | 1 | 2 | 3 | No limit |
| Size of each file | Up to 10 MB | Up to 25 MB | Up to 50 MB | Up to 100 MB |
| Version history | No | 1 month | 12 months | 12 months |
| Sites online with Blup | None | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| Custom domain on sites | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| AI (narration and translation) | No | Yes | Twice the Pro | Five times the Pro |
| Priority support | | | | Yes |

Every plan has **end-to-end encryption** and syncs as many vaults and sites as you want. The yearly plan costs ten months: two months are free.

> [!info] Custom domain on Blup Cloud
> A custom domain comes with the Max and Ultra plans and is linked only on Blup Cloud sites. With Cloudflare Workers and GitHub Pages, the site stays at the service's free address. See [Custom domain](../Publishing/Custom%20domain.md).

## See your usage

In **Settings > Blup Cloud**, bars show how much of the plan you use:

- **Storage**: how much of your notes is in the cloud (old versions count too).
- **AI this month**: how much you used, when it renews and how much you can still use.
- **Published sites** and **Computers**.

**See plans** shows the plans you can switch to, and **Manage account** opens your account in the browser.

## Without Blup Cloud

You can do almost everything for free, with other accounts:

- Sync your notes through a private GitHub repository.
- Publish on Cloudflare Workers or GitHub Pages, at the service's free address.
- Narrate and translate on your computer, or with your own OpenRouter key.
