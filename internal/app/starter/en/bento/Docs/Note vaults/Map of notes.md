The **Map of notes** draws the whole vault: each note is a dot and each link between notes is a line. Notes with more links are bigger.

Open it from the side strip or with `Ctrl+Alt+G` (the same shortcut takes you back to Write).

## Move around the map

- **Hover** over a dot: it and its neighbors light up.
- **Click** to open the note.
- **Drag a dot** to move it.
- **Drag an empty spot** to move the map and use the **mouse wheel** to zoom in and out.
- **See everything** fits the whole map. **Read the notes again** refreshes it.

The map also updates on its own when a note changes.

## Map settings

The corner panel has four parts. Everything is saved, and **Back to the default** undoes it.

| Part | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Filters | Search (lights up matching names), Folder, Tag, Tags as dots, Loose notes, Canvases and drawings, and **Around the open note**, with a depth of 1 to 4 links |
| Groups | Color the notes of a folder or tag |
| Display | Arrows, Text fade threshold, Note size, Line thickness and **Animate** (notes appear in the order they were created) |
| Forces | Center force, Repel force, Link force and Link distance |

> [!tip] What counts as a link
> Links between notes, `[[wikilinks]]`, note cards on a canvas and the **Link to a note** fields of databases. Drawings show up as dots, but they are not read for links.
