The **Note of the day** is the quickest way to jot something down without thinking about where to keep it.

## How to use it

Click **Note of the day** in the vault's side strip, or press `Ctrl+Alt+D`.

1. The Studio opens (or creates) today's note in the **Daily** folder, named after the date, like `2026-11-01`.
2. It adds a heading with the current time at the end, like `## 14:05`.
3. It puts the cursor right below, ready to write.

Each time you press it again on the same day, you get a new heading with the time. At the end of the day, the note is a record of what happened, in order.

## Tags

The note of the day starts with the **daily** tag, plus the tags most notes in the Daily folder already have.

> [!tip] Pair it with @
> Inside the note of the day, type `@` to mention a note ("talked to @Ana Lima") or a date. The mentioned note shows the note of the day in its **Mentioned in** panel.
