Click **Publish**, at the top or at the bottom of the side bar. The screen shows **Where to publish**, with Blup Cloud first.

## With Blup Cloud

1. Pick **Blup Cloud**. If you're not signed in, the Studio asks you to sign in. On the Free plan, it explains that this option comes with the paid plans and shows the plans.
2. In **Site address**, type the name: 3 to 40 lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.
3. As you type, the Studio checks if it is free: **Available**, **Already taken** (with similar names you can tap) or kept by Blup.
4. Click **Publish now**.

See the details in [Sites on Blup Cloud](../Blup%20Cloud/Sites%20on%20Blup%20Cloud.md).

## With Cloudflare Pages

1. Pick **Cloudflare Pages** and click **Connect the account**.
2. **Open in the browser** opens the key form, already with the right permissions. Click **Continue to summary** and then **Create Token**.
3. Paste the key and click **Connect**. It stays only on your computer.
4. Pick the account and the **Site name**: it becomes the address, `name.pages.dev`.
5. Click **Publish now**.

## With GitHub Pages

1. Pick **GitHub Pages** and click **Connect the account**.
2. **Open in the browser** opens the key form. In **Repository access**, pick **All repositories**, generate the key and copy it.
3. Paste the key and click **Connect**.
4. Pick the **Repository**: it becomes the address, `username.github.io/repository`.
5. Click **Publish now**.

## While it publishes

The screen shows each step. At the end, you see **Online at** with the address and the **Open the site** button. If something goes wrong, the details stay on the Publish screen.

> [!tip] The keys work for every site
> Connected accounts live in **Settings > Publishing**. You can disconnect them there too.

## After publishing

- To send your changes, click **Publish** again. See [Update the site](Update%20the%20site.md).
- With Cloudflare or GitHub, the **Custom domain** block shows up. See [Custom domain](Custom%20domain.md).
