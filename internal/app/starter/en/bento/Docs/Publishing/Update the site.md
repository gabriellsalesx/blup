The live site doesn't change by itself when you edit a note: it changes when you **publish again**. That way you can write calmly and decide when readers see it.

## With Blup Studio

1. Check the changes in the preview, next to the editor.
2. Click **Publish** and then **Publish now**.

## With the command line

Run `blup publish`.

## What happens

- Blup builds the whole site again on your computer.
- Only the files that changed are sent. A small update takes seconds.
- People who installed the site as an app get the new version on their own.

## What changes without publishing

Some things are read the moment the page opens, so they change without publishing:

- Votes, reactions and comments.
- The status of the requests on the Feedback board.

> [!tip] Changed the name, the menu or the domain?
> Everything that is a site setting also needs a new publish to go live.
