---
title: Preview
description: See the site in the browser while you write.
weight: 2
---
Run `blup preview`. Blup builds the site, opens the browser and keeps watching the folder: **each note you save in Obsidian updates the page on its own**, right on the page you're editing.

- The preview also shows drafts (`draft: true`).
- Posts with a password show up locked, just like on the real site.
- Search works in the preview.
- To stop, press **Ctrl+C** in the terminal.

> [!tip] In Blup Studio
> The Studio shows the preview next to the editor, with no terminal. See [Live preview](../Blup%20Studio/Live%20preview.md).

> [!tip] Keep it open
> With Obsidian on one side and the preview on the other, you see each change right away.
