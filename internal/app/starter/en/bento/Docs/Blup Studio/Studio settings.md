Open them with the gear at the bottom of the side strip, or with `Ctrl+,`. The settings apply to every site and vault on this computer.

![Blup setting things up](../../Attachments/blup-configurar.jpg)

## Blup Cloud

At the top sits the account card: your e-mail and plan, or **Sign in to Blup Cloud**. In the **Blup Cloud** tab you sign in and out, take care of the **notes password** and see your plan usage as bars: storage, AI this month, published sites and computers. There are also **See plans** and **Manage account**. See [Account and plans](../Blup%20Cloud/Account%20and%20plans.md).

## Studio

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| General | The Studio's language, **Open with the computer**, **Minimize and close to the tray** and the **Daily copy of the notes** |
| Appearance | Theme (light, dark or same as the system) and **Show names in the side menu** |
| Editor | Text size and width, spell checker, spell checker language, added words and **Open with the preview on** |
| Shortcuts | The Studio's shortcuts (change or turn off each one), the global shortcut and the list of editor shortcuts |
| Terminal | Text size and the **AIs in the terminal** that are installed |

> [!tip] Tray
> With the tray on, closing the window doesn't close the Studio: it stays as an icon, and terminals and AIs keep running. To quit for real, use **Quit** in the icon's menu.

## Sites

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Publishing | The GitHub and Cloudflare accounts Blup uses to publish, with **Connect** and **Disconnect** |
| General password | The password for posts marked with the general password, on every site. It stays only on this computer |

## AI and voice

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| AI | Blup Cloud's AI, the voice and translation on your computer, and the OpenRouter key |
| Narration | The default voice for each language (Português, English, Español), for every site |

See [AI in Blup Studio](../AI%20features/AI%20in%20Blup%20Studio.md).

## About

At the bottom, **Blup Studio** and the version open the **About** tab: version, what's new, **Check for updates**, the docs and the code. Blup is free and open source (AGPL-3.0 license).
