The Studio has several safety nets. Each one covers a different kind of mistake.

## Trash

Everything you delete in the Studio (notes, folders, attachments, authors and sections) goes to the **Trash**, at the end of the side strip.

- **Restore** puts the item back where it was. If something with the same name is already there, the Studio does not overwrite it.
- **Delete for good** removes one item forever.
- **Empty trash** removes everything forever.

The Trash lives in the site folder, outside your notes: it never goes to the site or to sync.

## Earlier versions (Blup Cloud)

With your notes synced through Blup Cloud on a plan with history, right-click a note and choose **Earlier versions**. Pick a version to see it and, if you want, **Bring this version back**. The current version stays in the history, so nothing is lost.

The Trash also shows **Deleted, kept on Blup Cloud**: deleted notes your plan's history still keeps, with **Bring back**. See [Version history](../Blup%20Cloud/Version%20history.md).

## Undo what the AI changed

Before an AI agent starts in the terminal, the Studio keeps a copy of your notes. Afterwards, **See what the AI changed** shows the new, changed and deleted notes, and **Undo all** puts the notes back as they were before the AI. See [Terminal and AI agents](Terminal%20and%20AI%20agents.md).

## Daily copy of the notes

In **Settings > General**, **Daily copy of the notes** saves one `.zip` a day of the notes of the open site or vault, in a folder you choose, and keeps the last 7 copies. **Copy now** makes a copy right away.

> [!tip] Pick a folder outside your notes
> An external drive or a folder from another cloud service are good choices. The copy is never kept inside the notes themselves.
