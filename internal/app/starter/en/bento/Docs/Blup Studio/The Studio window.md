When you open a site, the Studio shows the workspace. From left to right:

1. **Side strip**: the icons of the screens.
2. **Note tree**: the site's folders and notes.
3. **Editor**: the open note.
4. **Preview**: the real site, right beside it.
5. **Terminal** (optional): at the bottom, when you open it.

At the top are the **Preview**, **Sync** and **Publish** buttons.

![Blup in the terminal](../../Attachments/blup-terminal.jpg)

## The side strip

Hover over an icon to see its name. Prefer to always see the names? In **Settings > Appearance**, turn on **Show names in the side menu**.

On a **site**, the screens come in groups:

| Group | Screens |
| --- | --- |
| Content | Write, Attachments, Authors |
| Site | Site, Sections (Bento only), Languages |
| Audience | Comments, Feedback (in Bento, with the section) and Podcast (in blogs, with the section) |
| Privacy | Cookies and statistics |
| At the bottom | Publish, Trash and the terminal |

In a **note vault**, the strip changes: Write, Map of notes and Attachments; then New canvas, New drawing, New database and Note of the day; at the bottom, Sync, Trash and the terminal. See [What is a vault](../Note%20vaults/What%20is%20a%20vault.md).

At the bottom of the strip are **Help** (it opens these docs), the **gear** for [Studio settings](Studio%20settings.md) and the **site switcher**.

## The site switcher

Click it to switch to another site or vault without going back to the home screen. It separates **Sites** and **Vaults**, shows the recently opened ones first (up to five of each) and has **See all** and **Home screen**.

## The note tree

- **Files** shows the folders and notes. **Search** looks in the names and text of the notes, ignoring accents and capital letters, and shows the lines it found.
- **New note** and **New folder** create right there: a name field shows up inside the chosen folder; Enter creates it, Esc gives up.
- Click a folder to open or close it. It becomes the place for new notes.
- **Expand all** and **Collapse all** open and close every folder.

Right-click a note to open its menu:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| New note here, New folder here | Creates inside the folder |
| Rename | Changes the name. Links in other notes are fixed |
| Move to… | Picks another folder. Links are fixed too |
| Make a copy | Creates "Name 2" next to it |
| Copy note link | To paste into another note |
| Show in system folder | Opens the file manager |
| Export as HTML | A single page, with the images inside |
| Print or save as PDF | Opens the system's print dialog |
| Earlier versions | With Blup Cloud, shows the note's history |
| Delete | Goes to the Trash, and can be restored |

The authors and attachments folders don't show in the tree: they have their own screens, **Authors** and **Attachments**.

## Saving

The Studio saves on its own while you write, and before you switch notes or leave. The state shows on screen: saved, saving or not saved. `Ctrl+S` saves too.

If a note changes outside the Studio (in another editor or by an AI in the terminal), the tree refreshes. If you were editing that same note, the Studio lets you know and you choose between **Load the new one** and **Keep mine**.

## Main shortcuts

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| New note | `Ctrl+N` |
| Search notes | `Ctrl+Shift+F` |
| Show or hide the files panel | `Ctrl+Alt+S` |
| Turn the preview on or off | `Ctrl+Alt+P` |
| Open or close the terminal | Ctrl and the backtick key |
| Settings | `Ctrl+,` |

You can change or turn off any of them in **Settings > Shortcuts**.

> [!tip] The Studio from anywhere
> The global shortcut `Alt+Space` shows and hides Blup Studio even with another app in front. You can change it in the settings.
