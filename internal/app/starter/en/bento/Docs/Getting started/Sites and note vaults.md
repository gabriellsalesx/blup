Blup Studio works with two kinds of notes folder:

| | Site | Note vault |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What for | Publishing on the internet | Notes just for you, day to day |
| Theme and preview | Yes (Mochi, Nori or Bento) | No |
| Publish | Yes | No |
| Site screens (Site, Sections, Languages, Comments...) | Yes | No |
| Daily note, map of notes, canvas, drawings and databases | No | Yes |
| Sync between computers | Yes | Yes |
| Trash | Yes | Yes |

## The home screen

When you open the Studio, the home screen shows two lists: **Your sites** and **Note vaults**.

- Click a card to open it.
- **Create a new site** starts the step by step. See [Create your first site](Create%20your%20first%20site.md).
- **Create a note vault** only asks for the name and where the notes live. See [What is a vault](../Note%20vaults/What%20is%20a%20vault.md).
- Drag one card onto another to make a **folder** (like on a phone). Folders only organize the Studio: nothing changes in the files. A folder holds only sites or only vaults.
- With lots of items, **See all** shows the whole list with a search by name.
- The card's options button has **Remove from the list** (the files stay on your computer) and **Delete the site**.

> [!warning] Delete the site
> **Delete the site** lets you choose the parts: the site folder, the notes, the published site, the sync repository and the comments repository. Folders go to the computer's trash and can be restored; what is on the internet is gone for good. To confirm, the Studio asks you to type the site's name.

## Switch sites without going back home

At the bottom of the side strip sits the **site switcher**. It separates **Sites** and **Vaults**, shows the recently opened ones first and has **See all** and **Home screen**. See [The Studio window](../Blup%20Studio/The%20Studio%20window.md).
