The Studio translates the site's notes between Portuguese, English and Spanish. How translations become the site's pages in each language is in [Multilingual sites](../Site%20setup/Multilingual%20sites.md). This page is about **who translates**.

## The options

| Who translates | Cost | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On the computer** (Firefox Translations) | Free | Offline; your notes never leave the computer |
| **Blup Cloud AI** | Included in the paid plans | No key; uses the plan's AI for the month |
| **OpenRouter** | A few cents per note | With your own key |

In the cloud, translation usually reads a little more naturally.

## Set up translation on the computer

1. Open **Settings > AI and voice**.
2. Under **Translation**, click **Download**. It is about 100 MB, only once, and each file is checked.
3. When **Installed and ready** shows up, pick under **Who translates**: **On the computer** or **AI in the cloud**.

The computer translator works between English and Portuguese and between English and Spanish. From Portuguese to Spanish (and back), it goes through English.

## Use the cloud

- **With a paid Blup Cloud plan**: sign in to your account in **Settings > Blup Cloud**. Nothing else is needed.
- **With OpenRouter**: connect your key in **Settings > AI and voice > AI**. See [AI in Blup Studio](AI%20in%20Blup%20Studio.md).

With neither, the Studio tells you: "To translate, connect an OpenRouter key or use a Blup Cloud plan with AI."

## What is kept as it was

Code, links, images, math and the callout types come back exactly as they were. Long notes are translated in parts, and the Studio shows the progress.

> [!tip] Review it
> Every translation made by AI gets the **Translated with AI** badge on the site. Read the translation, adjust what you want and click **Mark as reviewed** to remove the badge.
