**Blup** turns your notes into a fast site, and it also keeps notes just for you, in **note vaults**. The recommended way to use it is **Blup Studio**, the app for your computer (Linux, macOS and Windows): you write, see the site preview beside the text and publish with one button.

The notes are plain Markdown files, so any text editor opens them too. To build and publish without the Studio, there is the `blup` command line.

## Highlights

- **AI built into the Studio**: a voice reads your posts, AI translates your notes and agents like Claude Code and Codex work on your notes from the terminal. Pay through your Blup Cloud plan, with your own OpenRouter key, or use the voice and the translation on your computer, for free.
- **Multilingual sites**: each note gets its translation in an `en`, `es` or `pt` folder next to it, and the site shows a language switcher at the top.
- **Podcast on blogs**: on the Mochi and Nori themes, narrations become episodes with a feed for Spotify and Apple Podcasts.

These docs are themselves an example: each page is a note in the **Docs** folder, and each group in the side menu is a subfolder.
